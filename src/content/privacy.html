Privacy policy

The Ferrite project collects nothing about you. The website has no accounts, cookies or analytics, and the software sends nothing to us.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This website

ferrite.me is a static website. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and we keep no record of who visits it. Its pages, fonts and pictures all come from ferrite.me itself.

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any host, it handles each request, including your IP address and browser, to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse, under its own privacy policy.

Links to GitHub and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies.

The demo

The demo runs entirely in your browser on made-up data and talks to no server. It keeps your theme, language and a pretend sign-in in your browser's local storage, which you can clear at any time.

The software

Ferrite runs on your own hardware and has no telemetry. We never receive your queries, logs, devices or settings, and we have no way to see them.

On your box, ferrite keeps what it needs to do its job: a log of the names each device looked up, statistics, device names and addresses, and the sessions of browsers signed in to the panel. All of it stays on the box. You choose how long it is kept, and you can switch the query log and statistics off.

To work, ferrite connects to other services:

  • GitHub, about once an hour to check for a new release and when it downloads one, so GitHub sees your box's IP address;
  • the servers of the blocklists you subscribe to (out of the box, one list on GitHub);
  • the upstream resolvers you choose (Google Public DNS until you change it), which see the names ferrite asks them about;
  • Let's Encrypt and Cloudflare, only if you let ferrite get a certificate;
  • the notification services, tunnels and proxies you set up yourself.

Each of them handles that under its own privacy policy.

If you run ferrite for other people

Whoever runs a ferrite can see the names the devices on it look up. If those devices belong to other people, such as family, flatmates or colleagues, you are the one handling their data: tell them, keep only what you need, and follow the privacy laws that apply to you. The Ferrite project has no access to your box and can't answer requests about the data on it.

Children

This website collects no information from anyone, children included. Profiles for children in ferrite are run by their parents on their own box, and that data stays there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date at the top.
